## Neutral invitation — draft only

Subject: A short conversation about translation project handovers?

Hello,

I'm exploring how small translation agencies coordinate projects between translators, reviewers and project managers. I'm at the research stage and don't have a product to sell. Would someone who handles these handovers be willing to spend 20 minutes talking through a recent job and what was difficult about coordinating it?

There's no need to share client names, documents or confidential email. A description of the sequence is enough. If this isn't relevant, no reply is needed.

Thank you,
[Founder name]

Do not send this until the founder explicitly authorizes contact and the appropriate general business route and any restrictions have been checked. No invented personalization or claimed relationship.

## Five-question guide

1. What's the hardest part of coordinating a job between the translator, reviewer and project manager?
2. Tell me about the last time you encountered that difficulty. What happened, in order?
3. Why was that difficult? What did it cost in time or change about the delivery?
4. What have you tried to handle it?
5. What do you dislike about those solutions?

Optional follow-ups only after their example: when did this happen; how often in the last month; who owned the deadline; where the change was recorded; how receipt was acknowledged; which tool was already available; what actually happened versus what nearly happened. Avoid asking whether they like our idea or would use hypothetical software.

If their hardest problem is something else, record it. Do not force their story into deadline changes. A colleague who genuinely fits can later participate, with relationship bias marked; the founder's current colleague anecdote is not that interview.
