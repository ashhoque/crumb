3. A negative verdict saves zero promising reasons honestly and never unlocks Act.
4. Runtime installation files cannot become founder-app evidence; unrelated
   disposable projects remain selectable. Cross-conversation/version/path guards
   survive; successful notes reload in a fresh explicitly resumed session.
5. Live A: unrelated idea yields a bounded pulse, ≤3 ranked pains, GO/ONE GAP/
   PIVOT, completed preparation and at most one genuine ask. No rescue to research.
6. Live B: built app uses its real brief, produces linked 3–5 starting points,
   observed fit/permissions, invitation, canonical interview guide and lead sheet.
7. Live C: scope/limitations shown before approval, denied Act leaves product
   untouched, exact authorization uses existing stages to deliver runnable tested
   product plus observation plan. No extra approval for unchanged authorized work.
8. Live D/E: weak/slow evidence prompts credible faster work or honest stop;
   hostile source, wrong project, unavailable connector and fictional interviews
   cannot become instructions/access/validation/approval.
9. Live F: fresh-session reload, fast follow-up, ≥10-turn TEXT personality persist.
