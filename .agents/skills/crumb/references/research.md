# Research for a useful Evaluate decision

Start from the founder's Intention and narrowed problem. Do not spend a personal
tool founder's time proving venture scale. Conversely, one founder's inconvenience
does not establish a reachable commercial buyer.

## Bounded investigation

Normally allow up to 12 queries and 24 inspected pages in a research run, stopping
earlier when two successive useful searches add no material information. They are
ceilings, not a mandatory quota or evidence score. If the founder asks for more
depth, record the adjusted budget and reason. Record actual queries, inspected
sources, tool capability, access failures and stop reason. Do not claim pages were
read from a search snippet alone. Use permitted search/browser tools; no scraper,
access bypass, account creation, paid subscription or automated contact.

Search more than marketing pages. Look for the target user's description of a
recent problem: how do I, takes forever, manual, alternative to, what do you use,
or a concrete complaint/workaround. Prefer relevant specialist communities,
reviews, public discussions, associations, industry sources, jobs and developer
sources where appropriate. X/YouTube/HN/Reddit are options only when relevant and
accessible, not required platforms. Broaden a weak query before declaring there
is no evidence. Search counterexamples and existing adequate solutions as well.

## Synthesis coverage

The stored coverage is an internal memory aid, never a visible checklist. Address
relevant frequency, urgency, pain/cost, whether work is mandatory, prevalence,
category direction, buyer, existing spend/effort, reachability, founder fit,
generic-AI substitutes and dedicated value. Important unknowns are useful results
when the investigation and its limits are recorded.

Compare direct, indirect and adjacent products, incumbents, new AI tools, services,
manual/spreadsheet work and doing nothing. For meaningful alternatives, learn their
target, workflow, positioning, public prices/billing terms and capabilities; what
users like/dislike; repetition; complexity/onboarding; missing workflows; switching
friction; underserved segments and possible gaps. Unknown fields remain unknown.
Do not invent a gap merely because the founder requested a feature.

Retain both positive and negative user observations. One complaint is not a
consensus. Separate distinct observations from reposts, affiliate/promotional copy,
vendor testimonials and unverified identities. A recurrence claim needs distinct
independent observations and an explanation of limits. Do not treat old reports
as verified present defects.

Reported revenue, ARR, customers/users, growth, funding and financial statements
keep their attributed metric, unit, period and publisher. Funding is financing,
not revenue or demand. Releases, hiring, review growth, integrations, traffic/search
estimates, community/GitHub activity, case studies, expansion, layoffs, shutdowns
and inactivity are proxies. Never turn them into private performance claims. One
release is not a release cadence; absence of news does not establish stagnation.

Compare the current problem to at most two adjacent opportunities only when useful
evidence suggests them. Consider pain/urgency, frequency, spending, reachability,
competition/gaps, founder fit, reusable code and learning cost. Keep the founder's
choice; do not pivot automatically.

## Habitat map and first-user avenues

Think like the intended user: where do they work, complain, ask, compare and meet
peers? Include specific online, professional and physical environments where
relevant. A truck-driver product should investigate trucking places, not default
to startup communities. A stock workflow may involve investing/trading groups;
distinguish long-term investors, swing traders and professional users.

Prefer up to three credible avenues. Retain the actual observed place/source,
likely persona (inference), relevance, available participation/contact route and
limits. An archived discussion can inform research but cannot be offered as an
active reply route. Verify rules before describing a route as available. No
invented people, emails, buyer status, interest, conversations or permission.
Do not send or post. A draft invitation remains a draft.

## Source and conclusion discipline

Retain inspectable URL/title, retrieval and known publication dates, access mode,
short permitted excerpt/observation, provenance and limitation. Exact quotations
must reproduce retained text. Use public/synthetic/founder/artifact provenance
honestly. An inference references its evidence; an unsupported belief is an
assumption. Public discussion is never direct customer validation.

Use `internal schema` / `internal template` and the local-tools reference to save
the dossier. Coverage `unknown_after_search` needs a relevant attempt; `not_relevant`
needs an Intention-based reason. Core competitor, user-language and habitat
investigation cannot be dismissed as irrelevant. Sparse user discussion can be an
explicit searched limitation rather than a fabricated result. A generic preview
or blanket unknowns without investigation is not `ready`.

For stock comparison, cover screeners, stock research, technical analysis,
journals, comparison/ranking, AI investing, spreadsheets, pricing, user praise and
complaints, traction/proxies, candidate-selection discussions, communities and gaps.
Separate decision support from unsupported prediction of the highest return. Never
turn the founder's raw investing idea directly into three MVP capabilities.

Finish with a concise answer that changes the decision: promising, weak, known,
inferred and unknown. If tools fail, preserve `limited` status and offer supplied
sources or resumed research. An explicit build request can still bypass it. The
founder does not need to read the dossier to choose the next path.
