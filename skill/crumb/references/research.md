# Research for a useful Evaluate decision

Use the decision policy before a new search: decision → missing external
evidence → materiality → bounded research → concise synthesis. A simple
clarification with no material external gap stays on the fast path: compact current
context, no history reload, repository scan or research call. When evidence matters,
reuse relevant current sources before searching. Start with a few authoritative
options, combine independent lookups when the host permits, and stop as soon as the
immediate decision is adequately supported. The `research-decision` Core helper
sets a small initial budget; the larger Evaluate ceilings below apply only when
the decision genuinely needs broader investigation. Record actual calls and
duplicate searches internally, never as routine user-facing diagnostics.

Money, competitor packaging, technical dependencies and first-user habitats are
common reasons to look ahead without being asked. They are not automatic browse
triggers. For material external numbers/current claims, retain source URL,
retrieval date, exact entity/product, relevant city/region and evidence status.
Call a vendor page publicly listed unless the current offer was directly verified.
Mark unsupported figures as estimates or unknowns and avoid false precision.
Search-result snippets alone are insufficient to quote a precise current price.
Open the vendor's pricing page when accessible and confirm the amount, unit,
plan and billing interval. If the host exposes only search results, avoid the
exact figure and give a qualitative comparison or an explicitly unverified
range instead. Keep the opening within the normal research page budget.

## Early Market Pulse and opportunity judgment

When a founder has a recognizable user, pain and product direction, do a small
Market Pulse before demanding full foundation confirmation if external evidence
could change the next recommendation. `market-pulse` sets a default three-query,
six-page batch; one follow-up batch is justified only by a material gap. Reuse
current relevant sources. Search public user discussions, reviews, issue trackers,
forums, relevant social results, vendor alternatives, jobs and industry sources
as appropriate. Private groups and login-gated results remain unavailable. A
search snippet is a lead to inspect, not an observed complaint.

Look for concentration of pain: frequency × intensity × economic consequence ×
segment concentration × existing spend. Keep these as separate observations,
not a made-up score. Distinguish who reports pain, who pays, what they do now,
whether an incumbent solves it adequately, and whether we can reach them. A
high-engagement post establishes engagement on that post, not market size, growth,
or willingness to pay. An individual price or complaint is not category demand.
If evidence is thin, say so and propose a direct user test.

Use `opportunity-map` as an internal bounded synthesis when useful. Separate
inspected observations, inference, hypothesis and Crumb's recommendation. Its
market-size output remains unknown because public examples cannot establish TAM.
Look for adjacent problems and segments where the current product has a sharper
fit, but compare at most two and explain why a small test beats a premature pivot.
For an existing app/pricing question, inspect the smallest relevant files first,
then fill the material external gap; do not restart onboarding.

Keep research answers conversational: one useful finding, the strongest caveat,
Crumb's opinion and one next move. Bring some energy to a real signal, but do not
turn it into a victory lap before a user has acted.

Start bounded early investigation when the intended user and pain are plausible,
even before formal foundation confirmation. Mark them provisional, revise them
from evidence and never use the pulse as Act approval. Full Evaluation review and
Act still require the current founder-confirmed foundations. Do not spend a
personal-tool founder's time proving venture scale. Conversely, one founder's
inconvenience does not establish a reachable commercial buyer.

Crumb owns this investigation. Use `internal evaluation-plan` only for formal Evaluation preparation or
a material refresh, reuse current sources, and save populated `quality` alongside
the dossier. The tool plans/validates; it does not browse. Actually use permitted
host research tools. Never ask the founder which API, database, competitors or
communities to investigate. Propose supported options, then confirm intent.

## Technical feasibility, without an engineering report in chat

Investigate data/APIs; freshness/quality; licensing/access; rate limits, pricing
and operating cost; integrations; complexity/latency; model suitability and
deterministic alternatives; reliability/failures; security/privacy; vendor dependence
and build versus buy. Inspect permitted existing code first. Never inspect secrets.

`quality.technical` retains a concise summary, central risk, up to three options,
a reasoned recommendation and source/run-linked coverage. Unknown after research
differs from unexamined. Explain irrelevance. Vendor docs do not prove runtime
reliability; record needed authorized checks honestly. No invented prices/benchmarks.
Show the judgment and main constraint briefly, then **View technical evaluation**.
Prepare reversible briefs and concept drafts before Act; actual product implementation requires the applicable Act approval. Delayed holdings, for example,
describe a reported period, not current buying. Confirm whether that fits intent.

## Startup discipline in decisions

Use prevalence/growth, urgency, consequence, necessity and frequency as lenses,
not requirements every opportunity must satisfy or a score. Connect buyer/payment,
switching, reachability and AI substitution to the decision. `quality.founderFit`
records a reported/supported edge, its limits and claims. Experience, code and
access may help; none establishes demand or an unfair advantage.

Choose the fastest credible learning path that can materially improve the next decision while minimizing founder effort. Compare elapsed time, founder labour, evidence quality, relevance, reversibility and monetary/tool cost. Recommend direct conversations for
lived-behavior uncertainty rather than endless desk research. Interviews remain
optional. After explicit Act, compare manual/existing-tool delivery before adding
automation; keep the smallest complete path that tests the pain. No YC lecture.
See [Kevin Hale's problem lenses](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/startup-school-podcast-preview-with-kevin-hale)
and [YC's early-stage advice](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/ycs-essential-startup-advice/).

## Bounded investigation

When direct outcome validation takes weeks/months, check public/historical evidence,
retrospective analysis, valid simulations/backtests/benchmarks, observed competitor
behavior, focused conversations and honest mockup/concierge proxies. Explain what
the faster evidence can establish, cannot establish and what direct validation must
still prove. Run the slow track in parallel with reversible research, pricing, user
discovery and preparation; prototypes require the applicable Act approval. Never
turn a simulation or proxy into proven retention, payment, safety or demand.

Normally allow up to 12 queries and 24 inspected pages in a research run, stopping
earlier when two successive useful searches add no material information. They are
ceilings, not a mandatory quota or evidence score. If the founder asks for more
depth, record the adjusted budget and reason. Record actual queries, inspected
sources, tool capability, access failures and stop reason. Do not claim pages were
read from a search snippet alone. Use permitted search/browser tools; no scraper,
access bypass, account creation, paid subscription or automated contact.

Search more than marketing pages. Look for the target user's description of a
recent problem: how do I, takes forever, manual, alternative to, what do you use,
or a concrete complaint/workaround. Prefer relevant specialist communities,
reviews, public discussions, associations, industry sources, jobs and developer
sources where appropriate. X/YouTube/HN/Reddit are options only when relevant and
accessible, not required platforms. Broaden a weak query before declaring there
is no evidence. Search counterexamples and existing adequate solutions as well.

## Synthesis coverage

The stored coverage is an internal memory aid, never a visible checklist. Address
relevant frequency, urgency, pain/cost, whether work is mandatory, prevalence,
category direction, buyer, existing spend/effort, reachability, founder fit,
generic-AI substitutes and dedicated value. Important unknowns are useful results
when the investigation and its limits are recorded.

Compare direct, indirect and adjacent products, incumbents, new AI tools, services,
manual/spreadsheet work and doing nothing. For meaningful alternatives, learn their
target, workflow, positioning, public prices/billing terms and capabilities; what
users like/dislike; repetition; complexity/onboarding; missing workflows; switching
friction; underserved segments and possible gaps. Unknown fields remain unknown.
Do not invent a gap merely because the founder requested a feature.

Retain both positive and negative user observations. One complaint is not a
consensus. Separate distinct observations from reposts, affiliate/promotional copy,
vendor testimonials and unverified identities. A recurrence claim needs distinct
independent observations and an explanation of limits. Do not treat old reports
as verified present defects.

Reported revenue, ARR, customers/users, growth, funding and financial statements
keep their attributed metric, unit, period and publisher. Funding is financing,
not revenue or demand. Releases, hiring, review growth, integrations, traffic/search
estimates, community/GitHub activity, case studies, expansion, layoffs, shutdowns
and inactivity are proxies. Never turn them into private performance claims. One
release is not a release cadence; absence of news does not establish stagnation.

Compare the current problem to at most two adjacent opportunities only when useful
evidence suggests them. Consider pain/urgency, frequency, spending, reachability,
competition/gaps, founder fit, reusable code and learning cost. Keep the founder's
choice; do not pivot automatically.

## Habitat map and first-user avenues

Activate these automatically whenever customer evidence blocks the next decision,
including provisional users before formal review. Complete the First User Pack
defined in SKILL.md; surface inspected direct URLs in that answer, not a hidden
detail view. Do not wait for a request to find people or draft outreach.

For an earlier first-user request, use `first-user-plan` and `discovery-plan` to
pick one or two research habitats and one or two acquisition routes. The former
shows where people discuss pain; the latter is where founder-controlled contact
may be appropriate after rules and permissions are checked. Start locally for a
local business market and ask for city/region if absent. Start in focused online
communities for a distributed role. Review roughly 5–20 high-fit public
candidates when available; shortlist roughly five and present normally 3–5 strong
starting points. Keep the three best source-backed cards in compact
context. Named people are optional, and an inaccessible route is not a lead.
When user contact is the bottleneck, inspect public candidate pages yourself and give
the best verified matches with observed fit and limits. Do not delegate the
shortlist search back to the founder. If only a few can be verified within the
bounded batch, return those few and say the shortlist is incomplete.
Observed public business details may support a route; do not invent contact
details or infer interest. `outreach-draft` produces a short founder-review draft
only; it cannot send. An observed like or reply count may be reported with source,
but never estimated from social buzz.

Think like the intended user: where do they work, complain, ask, compare and meet
peers? Include specific online, professional and physical environments where
relevant. A truck-driver product should investigate trucking places, not default
to startup communities. A stock workflow may involve investing/trading groups;
distinguish long-term investors, swing traders and professional users.

Prefer up to three credible avenues. Retain the actual observed place/source,
likely persona (inference), relevance, available participation/contact route and
limits. An archived discussion can inform research but cannot be offered as an
active reply route. Verify rules before describing a route as available. No
invented people, emails, buyer status, interest, conversations or permission.
Do not send or post. A draft invitation remains a draft.

Close Evaluate with a few strong first-user avenues without waiting to be asked.
Reuse current findings first; `discovery-plan` bounds a justified refresh to normally
six queries and ten pages. Consider pain discussions, professional associations,
directories, local businesses/events when relevant, and named public professionals
or handles only where collection/use is permitted. Ask one missing audience or
city/region question; never infer an address or locality. A snippet is not an
inspected discussion, an inaccessible group is not evidence about its members,
and a public URL is not commercial collection or outreach permission. Rank fit to
the task, not fame. Keep satisfied users/counterevidence when showing complaints.
No results describe coverage limits, not absence of demand. Preserve opt-outs.

## Source and conclusion discipline

Retain inspectable URL/title, retrieval and known publication dates, access mode,
short permitted excerpt/observation, provenance and limitation. Exact quotations
must reproduce retained text. Use public/synthetic/founder/artifact provenance
honestly. An inference references its evidence; an unsupported belief is an
assumption. Public discussion is never direct customer validation.

Use `internal schema` / `internal template` and the local-tools reference to save
the dossier. Coverage `unknown_after_search` needs a relevant attempt; `not_relevant`
needs an Intention-based reason. Core competitor, user-language and habitat
investigation cannot be dismissed as irrelevant. Sparse user discussion can be an
explicit searched limitation rather than a fabricated result. A generic preview
or blanket unknowns without investigation is not `ready`.

For stock comparison, cover screeners, stock research, technical analysis,
journals, comparison/ranking, AI investing, spreadsheets, pricing, user praise and
complaints, traction/proxies, candidate-selection discussions, communities and gaps.
Separate decision support from unsupported prediction of the highest return. Never
turn the founder's raw investing idea directly into three MVP capabilities.

Finish with a concise answer that changes the decision: promising, weak, known,
inferred and unknown. If tools fail, preserve `limited` status and offer supplied
sources or resumed research. It cannot unlock Act; supplied sources or resumed research can continue IDE. The
founder does not need to read the dossier to choose the next path.
