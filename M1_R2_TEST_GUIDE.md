Judge whether the early response makes the problem clearer and uses your goal.
It should usually ask one useful question, avoid a questionnaire, and avoid making
a feature list or interview assignment the default first value.

Let Crumb investigate on the research path. It should explain what it learned,
what challenges the idea and what remains unknown. Open optional detail and then
continue without reading everything. Returning later should retain your answers.
Sparse findings and unavailable pages should remain explicit.

After evaluation, try the interview or MVP option. Request the optional startup
master or skip it. Correct important facts and check that understanding changes
and old unapproved scope is retired. Explicit build should skip business homework
while showing a small concrete scope before approval.

Report where it felt premature, repetitive, vague, too long, or less useful than
ordinary conversation. Automated checks and synthetic rehearsals do not substitute
for your acceptance. This trial starts no later milestone.
